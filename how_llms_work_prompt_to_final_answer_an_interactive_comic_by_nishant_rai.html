Pipeline Stage 🎈 ELI5 Metaphor 🎓 Undergrad CS Concept 🔬 Tensor Shape & Formula
1. Tokenization (BPE) Snapping Lego syllables together and stamping an ID number on each brick Byte-Pair Encoding maps raw UTF-8 strings into discrete vocabulary indices String → t ∈ ℤ^[B, S], |V| ≈ 128,256
2. Embeddings + RoPE Placing each word on a 4,096-D star map and twisting a clock hand for word order Lookup table W_E plus Rotary Positional Embeddings in Query/Key subspaces X_0 = W_E[t] ∈ ℝ^[B, S, d_model], R_Θ(m)
3. Self-Attention Words at a cocktail party shining flashlights on earlier words to gather context Scaled Dot-Product Attention with Causal Masking & Grouped-Query Attention (GQA) softmax((QK^T + M) / √d_k) V
4. MLP & Residual Stream A shared conveyor belt visiting a giant library of factual reflex neurons Pre-RMSNorm residual connections + SwiGLU Feed-Forward Key-Value memories x + W_down(SiLU(W_gate x) ⊙ W_up x)
5. Prefill & KV Cache Writing notes on a sticky pad while reading the prompt so you never re-read old pages Compute-bound parallel Prefill (TTFT) vs. Memory-bound sequential Decode (ITL) Cache: 2 × L × n_kv × d_h × S × bytes
6. Logits & Sampling Scoring every word in the dictionary, turning a creativity knob, and rolling loaded dice Unembedding projection W_U, Temperature scaling, Softmax, Top-k & Nucleus (Top-p) P(w_i) = exp(z_i / T) / Σ_j exp(z_j / T)
7. Loop, CoT & RLHF Feeding each spoken word back into your ear & writing scratchpad math steps out loud Autoregressive loop until EOS token, Chain-of-Thought compute scaling, SFT + DPO/RLHF P(Y|X) = Π_t P(y_t | X, y_<t)
