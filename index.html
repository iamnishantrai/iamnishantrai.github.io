EASIEST • 60 SECONDS IN BROWSER

Option A: Drag & Drop on GitHub.com

  1. Click "⬇️ Download Ready `index.html`" above (it automatically stamps your name and settings into the file).
  2. Go to github.com/new ↗ and create a Public repository named how-netflix-works.
  3. Click "uploading an existing file" on the repo setup screen, drag in your downloaded index.html (and optional README.md), and click Commit changes.
  4. In your repo, go to Settings → Pages (left sidebar).
  5. Under Build and deployment → Branch, select main and folder / (root), then click Save.
  6. Wait ~45 seconds and visit your live comic site! 🎉
